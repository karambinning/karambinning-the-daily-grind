A note

52 London Road, King’s Lynn

Six or seven tables. Mandy at the counter. Cups of coffee that take their time, and a kitchen that cooks everything to order, never before.

The Daily Grind is small on purpose. The room holds half a dozen tables, the placemats are made from saved vinyl records, and the wall behind the counter is hung with historical photographs of London Road through the ages — a long quiet conversation between today’s customers and yesterday’s shopkeepers.

Baguettes are cooked when you order them, wholemeal or white. Soups are homemade. Salads are made up with fresh local ingredients. The coffee is the kind people come back for. None of it is fast. All of it is the point.

Most days, the regulars arrive first and stay longest. Visitors to the Travelodge across the way wander in for breakfast and quietly decide they’ll come back tomorrow too. Bring a friend, bring a paper, bring an hour — that’s the only ask.
